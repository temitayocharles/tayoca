# Tayoca Automated Literature Revenue Engine

## Goal
Create at least 18 pain-point-led literature assets per campaign, route every asset through approval, publish to approved channels, and connect every asset to a measurable revenue path.

## Campaign output
Each source problem produces: one pillar article, three SEO articles, three LinkedIn posts, three short-form scripts, two email editions, two checklists, one lead magnet, one product offer, one case-study angle, and one WhatsApp conversation prompt.

## Workflow stages
1. Pain-point intake from CRM, search data, WhatsApp, sales calls, and social comments.
2. Opportunity scoring by urgency, buyer intent, commercial value, and evidence availability.
3. Research pack with primary sources and customer-language excerpts.
4. Generate 18+ channel-specific assets from one approved campaign brief.
5. Human approval gate before external publication.
6. Publish only to approved platforms.
7. Add canonical URLs, UTMs, product/service CTA, and WhatsApp CTA.
8. Record publication, engagement, leads, purchases, and booked calls in the CRM.
9. Feed conversion results back into topic scoring.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated customer evidence or statistics.
- No automatic publishing without an explicit approved status.
- No duplicate cross-posting without canonical attribution.
- Every asset must solve a specific pain point and have one primary conversion action.
- Failed publications retry safely and never create duplicate posts.

## Required n8n credentials
Notion or Airtable, Tayoca CMS/GitHub, LinkedIn, approved publishing platforms, email provider, WhatsApp/Sivanta, CRM, analytics, and image generation provider.
