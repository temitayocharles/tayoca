## Locked positioning

Tayoca is an **engineering-led technology value company** that makes complex technology **reliable, economically accountable, secure, and operationally scalable**.

We do not sell technology for its own sake. We diagnose expensive operating problems, establish evidence, implement the smallest useful intervention, and measure the result.

## Initial buyer segments

The locked initial market segments are:

1. **Growth-stage technology companies** — organizations scaling digital products, engineering teams, cloud/platform estates, or AI workloads where reliability, cost, and operating discipline must mature faster than complexity.
2. **Regulated or operationally sensitive organizations** — organizations where reliability, recovery, security, evidence, governance, or controlled change materially affect business risk.
3. **AI-enabled businesses and engineering teams** — organizations deploying AI infrastructure, automation, agents, or AI-enabled products that need measurable economics, safe operations, observability, and human control.

### Accountable buyer roles

- **Economic and technology value owners:** CFO, CTO, FinOps lead, cloud/platform owner.
- **Engineering and platform leaders:** CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Platform, SRE/DevOps lead.
- **Operations and automation leaders:** COO, operations leader, product/service owner, automation or AI lead.

These roles are buying personas inside the three market segments above; they do not replace the locked segment definition.

## Three core practices

1. **Technology Value & FinOps** — cloud and AI economics, cost accountability, unit economics, optimization, and value realization.
2. **Platform Reliability & DevSecOps** — GitOps, delivery controls, Kubernetes/platform engineering, observability, recovery, security, and operational readiness.
3. **Agentic Operations & Automation** — workflow automation, AI agents, RAG/LLMOps, integration, observability, human approval, and controlled execution.

The practices may combine inside one engagement, but every engagement must have one primary business problem and one accountable outcome.

## Engagement ladder

1. **Free maturity signal** — lightweight self-assessment or maturity signal used for education and lead qualification; it must not imply a paid diagnostic has occurred.
2. **Qualified diagnostic** — bounded fit/decision conversation used to identify the decision, evidence access, urgency, and correct next step.
3. **Fixed-price evidence-based assessment** — one of the three canonical assessments below, delivered against an agreed evidence boundary.
4. **Fixed-scope remediation sprint** — bounded implementation with explicit acceptance criteria, dependencies, rollback/handover, and change control.
5. **Milestone-based transformation** — larger delivery split into accepted outcomes and milestones rather than unbounded time-and-materials work.
6. **Managed operation** — recurring optimization, reliability, platform, or automation operation with service boundaries, review cadence, ownership, and exit conditions.
7. **Selective outcome-based arrangement** — used only where baseline, attribution, measurement, dependencies, and verification are defensible.

## Offer contract

No offer is commercially complete unless it states all seven locked gate fields below:

1. **Buyer** — who owns the problem and can approve the work.
2. **Expensive problem** — the observable business or operating condition creating material cost, risk, delay, lost capacity, or control failure.
3. **Outcome** — the decision, capability, or measurable improvement the engagement is intended to create.
4. **Evidence requirement** — evidence needed to establish the baseline and verify any result claim.
5. **Scope** — inclusions, exclusions, acceptance criteria, handover, dependencies, and change-control boundary.
6. **Delivery model** — how the work is commercially and operationally delivered: fixed-price assessment, fixed-scope sprint, milestone transformation, managed operation, or explicitly governed outcome-based arrangement.
7. **CTA** — one explicit next action.

If any field is absent, the offer remains draft and must not be promoted as production-ready.

## Canonical offers

### Technology Value Assessment

- **Buyer:** executive technology/business owner with interconnected cost, reliability, delivery, or automation concerns.
- **Expensive problem:** leadership lacks one evidence-backed view of where technology is creating value, risk, avoidable spend, delivery friction, or operational exposure.
- **Outcome:** executive decision brief, value/risk map, maturity view, and prioritized 90-day roadmap.
- **Evidence requirement:** agreed evidence sample spanning relevant billing, delivery, reliability, control, and workflow data. Findings must distinguish observed facts from estimates and recommendations.
- **Scope:** decision framing, bounded evidence review, analysis, executive brief, technical evidence pack, prioritized roadmap, and readout. Implementation excluded unless separately scoped.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-price evidence-based assessment.
- **CTA:** `https://tayoca.com/assessments.html` — Start an Assessment.

### Cloud & AI Cost Assessment

- **Buyer:** CFO, CTO, FinOps lead, cloud/platform owner.
- **Expensive problem:** cloud or AI spend is rising without clear cost drivers, ownership, unit economics, or a defensible optimization backlog.
- **Outcome:** cost-driver map, savings hypotheses, accountability model, and prioritized action plan.
- **Evidence requirement:** actual billing/cost exports, relevant workload context, ownership/allocation data where available, and explicit confidence levels for modeled savings.
- **Scope:** bounded cost/evidence review and recommendations. Savings are hypotheses until implemented and verified against an agreed baseline.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-price evidence-based assessment.
- **CTA:** `https://tayoca.com/assessments.html` — Start an Assessment.

### Platform Reliability Assessment

- **Buyer:** CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Platform, SRE/DevOps lead.
- **Expensive problem:** deployments, incidents, recovery, security controls, or platform governance create material delivery risk, lost engineering capacity, customer exposure, or operational instability.
- **Outcome:** reliability findings, control gaps, recovery evidence, and prioritized remediation backlog.
- **Evidence requirement:** relevant CI/CD, platform, observability, incident, recovery, and control evidence; no reliability claim may be inferred from architecture diagrams alone.
- **Scope:** bounded technical assessment and remediation plan. Production changes excluded unless separately scoped.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-price evidence-based assessment.
- **CTA:** `https://tayoca.com/assessments.html` — Start an Assessment.

### Cloud Cost Optimization Engagement

- **Buyer:** economic/technology value owner responsible for cloud spend.
- **Expensive problem:** material cloud waste or poor cost accountability is suspected and the organization needs an actionable remediation path.
- **Outcome:** prioritized optimization backlog with owners, expected economic impact, implementation order, and validation method.
- **Evidence requirement:** source billing data and workload evidence; realized-savings claims require post-change billing validation against the agreed baseline.
- **Scope:** current public baseline is a 90-minute deep dive plus analysis and a prioritized remediation plan. Any implementation is separately scoped unless the signed engagement says otherwise.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-scope remediation engagement; expand to milestone delivery only through an approved scope change.
- **Pricing:** current public anchor is **$5,000 flat fee**. Currency, taxes, evidence-access assumptions, and any guarantee must be explicit in the signed scope.

### GitOps & DevSecOps Platform Engagement

- **Buyer:** engineering/platform leader accountable for delivery reliability and platform operations.
- **Expensive problem:** the team lacks a reliable, observable, secure delivery platform or has material platform/recovery gaps that cause delivery delay, incident exposure, or excessive operational toil.
- **Outcome:** a bounded production platform capability with documented controls, operability, handover, and acceptance evidence.
- **Evidence requirement:** agreed architecture and control evidence plus testable delivery/recovery acceptance criteria. Uptime/SLA claims require measured evidence and an explicit service boundary.
- **Scope:** discovery, target architecture, agreed implementation, validation, runbooks, and handover. Scope changes require written change control.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-scope remediation sprint or milestone-based transformation, selected from the approved scope.
- **Pricing:** current public anchor is **$8,000+ fixed scope**, with milestone payments for larger delivery.

### AI Automation / RAG / LLMOps Pilot

- **Buyer:** operations or technology leader who owns a repetitive workflow or AI-enabled business process.
- **Expensive problem:** manual work is expensive or slow, or existing AI automation is unreliable, opaque, unsafe, or difficult to operate.
- **Outcome:** a bounded automation capability with measurable acceptance criteria, observability, failure handling, and explicit human handoff where judgment is required.
- **Evidence requirement:** pre-pilot workflow baseline plus acceptance tests for task quality, failure behavior, latency/cost where relevant, and human escalation. Business-value claims require measured before/after evidence.
- **Scope:** discovery, workflow/model design, pilot implementation, validation, runbooks, and handover. Production scale-out is separately scoped unless included in the signed engagement.
- **Delivery model:** fixed-scope pilot; successful pilots may progress to milestone transformation or managed operation under a separate approved scope.
- **Pricing:** current public anchor is **$5,000 starting package**; final price follows evidence access, integrations, risk, and acceptance scope.
- **CTA:** `https://cal.com/tayoca/platform-engineering-consultation` — Book AI Pilot.

## Pricing and engagement policy

- Diagnose before prescribing. A qualified diagnostic may precede any paid scope.
- Prefer fixed-price assessments and fixed-scope delivery when inputs, acceptance criteria, and dependencies can be bounded.
- Use milestone payments for larger implementation work; tie milestones to deliverables and acceptance evidence, not elapsed time alone.
- Ongoing managed operations require a defined baseline, service boundary, cadence, ownership, and exit terms.
- Outcome-based pricing is selective and requires defensible baseline, attribution, measurement, dependencies, and verification.
- Third-party cloud, SaaS, model/API, licensing, tax, and procurement costs are excluded unless the signed scope explicitly includes them.
- Estimates and modeled savings must state assumptions and confidence; they are not realized outcomes.
- Guarantees, refunds, SLAs, delivery-time promises, and performance commitments are valid only when their conditions and measurement method are written into the approved offer/SOW.
- Scope changes require documented impact to price, schedule, risk, and acceptance criteria before work expands.
- No private-repository detail, client-confidential evidence, credential, or restricted operational information may be used as sales proof without explicit authorization.
