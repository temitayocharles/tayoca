# Community Website Acquisition Strategy

Status: authoritative Community Website Initiative operating policy

Recorded: 2026-08-30

## Commercial objective

The Community Website Initiative exists to create qualified conversations, earn trust, deliver measurable value, and convert appropriate relationships into testimonials, referrals, case studies, and repeatable Tayoca revenue.

Outreach must optimize for actual response behavior, not for an abstract preference for permission-first messaging.

## Two valid acquisition modes

### 1. Permission-first outreach

Use when the business identity/contact is verified but the evidence does not justify sending an unsolicited concept immediately, or when the channel/context makes a lower-friction permission ask more appropriate.

Typical sequence:

research -> verify identity/contact -> send permission-first message -> await response -> reveal concept after positive permission

### 2. Concept-first magnet outreach

A fully built concept may be sent immediately when evidence supports that doing so is likely to create a materially stronger acquisition magnet than a text-only permission request.

Typical sequence:

research -> verify identity/contact -> build concept -> QA -> deploy bounded preview -> capture landing-page screenshot -> send concise message + screenshot + preview link -> measure response

This mode is explicitly allowed because many local-business owners may not reliably respond to generic outreach emails or DMs. A tangible visual artifact can communicate value more effectively than asking them to imagine it.

## Concept-first eligibility gate

Before sending a concept, verify all of the following:

- exact business identity is reconciled;
- business is currently active;
- owned-site status is checked immediately before outreach;
- business-owned contact route is verified;
- concept uses only verified business facts;
- no invented pricing, hours, inventory, availability, warranties, certifications, reviews, financing, promotions, outcomes, or service claims;
- no copied customer imagery, reviews, or copyrighted third-party assets without permission;
- concept is clearly identified as an independent Tayoca concept, not the official business website;
- no impersonation or implied affiliation;
- the preview is safe to send and technically functional;
- outreach ledger and anti-duplication state are updated immediately after sending.

## Preview/deployment policy

Concept-first outreach may use a deployed Vercel preview as the magnet. A preview URL being reachable for evaluation does not make the concept the business's official website and does not authorize Tayoca to represent the business publicly.

Where practical, keep preview surfaces de-indexed or otherwise excluded from search discovery until the business has accepted delivery or explicitly authorized public use.

A landing-page screenshot should normally accompany the link when the messaging channel supports images. The screenshot is a conversion asset: it lets the owner understand the value before deciding whether to click.

## Build authority and handoff

Website construction and Vercel deployment for this initiative are owned by the user's Hermes Agent workflow, which has the required site-building skills and design instructions.

ChatGPT/Tayoca operating responsibilities are downstream and supervisory:

- choose and research candidates;
- determine whether permission-first or concept-first is the stronger bounded experiment;
- provide verified facts and guardrails where needed;
- inspect the work Hermes produced after completion;
- verify factual integrity, identity, disclosure, accessibility, responsiveness, links, obvious quality issues, and deployment state;
- reconcile the resulting asset, URL, screenshot, outreach, response, and outcome into Growth OS and canonical Tayoca state;
- do not rebuild the site independently merely to duplicate Hermes' work.

## Experiment discipline

Do not turn concept-first outreach into an indiscriminate blast.

Treat each send as a bounded acquisition experiment. Track at minimum:

- prospect;
- outreach mode: permission_first or concept_first;
- channel;
- message variant;
- screenshot included;
- preview link included;
- sent timestamp;
- delivery state where observable;
- reply classification;
- qualified conversation;
- positive permission / interest;
- meeting or next step;
- delivery outcome;
- testimonial/referral/case-study outcome when earned;
- revenue attribution where applicable.

Compare response and conversion rates by outreach mode. Allow evidence to determine which mode becomes the default for each prospect class and channel.

## Safety boundary

Concept-first means showing real work sooner. It does not mean fabricating business facts, impersonating the business, publishing an official site without authority, or bypassing identity/contact verification.

Evidence before claims. Control before scale.
