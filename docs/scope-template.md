# Tayoca Scope Template

Use this scope with the Stage 11 proposal record. The scope is versioned and tied to one opportunity. It does not expand automatically because a related system, repository, account, or business problem is discovered during delivery.

## Change control

A material change requires:

1. the requested change and reason;
2. affected deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, or price;
3. a new scope version;
4. approval before work proceeds on the changed scope.

The previous scope version remains part of the commercial history and is not silently overwritten.

## Security, confidentiality, and disclosure

Existing Tayoca evidence and disclosure controls remain binding. Private repositories and restricted operational evidence are non-public by default. Any later public case study, article, testimonial, or sales asset requires its own evidence/disclosure approval and is not authorized by engagement acceptance.
