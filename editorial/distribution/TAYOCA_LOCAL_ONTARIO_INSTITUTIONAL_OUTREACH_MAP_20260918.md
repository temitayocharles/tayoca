- Do not imply municipal, county, Board of Trade, or Small Business Centre endorsement.
- Do not imply an existing partnership.
- Do not send bulk templated outreach.
- Keep Community Website Initiative terms accurate.
- Do not promise unlimited free work.
- Do not purchase memberships, sponsorships, ads, or event placements without explicit approval.
- Record any actual outreach and response in the Growth OS before follow-up.
- No em dash punctuation in public copy.
