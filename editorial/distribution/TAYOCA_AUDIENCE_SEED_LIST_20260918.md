## Interaction policy

- Do not mass-follow indiscriminately.
- Prioritize institutions, communities, practitioners and buyers relevant to Tayoca's actual work.
- No automated comments, unsolicited promotional replies or spam.
- Local groups should be used for useful participation first, promotion second.
- Technical accounts should be followed for ecosystem awareness and authentic participation.
- Any group promotion must follow the current group rules.
- No em dash punctuation in Tayoca public copy.

## Current execution constraint

The connected Instagram and Facebook APIs expose publishing, profile, analytics and page-management actions but do not expose follow, unfollow, Page-like, or group-join actions. Reddit's connected action set also does not expose subreddit subscription.

Therefore this seed list is ready for manual follow/join execution in the platform UIs. The next growth stage should not assume these follows were completed until they are confirmed.
