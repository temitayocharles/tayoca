# Tayoca Community Website Initiative: Shelburne Candidate Research

Date: 2026-09-20

Status: research only

This document is not an outreach list and does not authorize contact. A business appears here only when public evidence suggests that its dedicated web presence may be limited or unclear. Before any outreach, the business must be rechecked and the contact path must be verified.

## Qualification rules

A strong candidate should satisfy most of the following:

- independently operated or locally owned
- serves customers in or near Shelburne, Ontario
- public evidence of an operating business
- no clearly maintained dedicated website, or a web presence primarily dependent on social/directory pages
- a standalone site could plausibly improve discovery, service/menu clarity, contact, booking, ordering, or trust
- no claim is made that the business lacks a website unless current public evidence supports that statement
