Giving AI access to evidence is not the same as giving AI authority to publish it.

Tayoca's publication control plane separates discovery, verification, disclosure classification, human approval, publication and correction.

Before any post is published, all of the following must be true:

2. The final body matches the approved claim scope.
3. The final asset is the approved asset.
4. The landing URL is live at distribution time.
5. No em dash punctuation appears anywhere in the public copy.
6. No new factual claim has been introduced during channel adaptation.
7. Publication URL is written back to Growth OS after distribution.
