AI Made Simple

A 30-words-explained educational manuscript and course kit to make AI concepts simple, practical, and actionable.

AI Made Simple condenses complex AI concepts into 30 plain words, making them actionable and memorable for students, mid-career professionals, and lifelong learners. Perfect for classrooms, upskilling, or everyday curiosity.

  • It shows what each concept is in ≤30 words
  • It explains why it matters in real words
  • It gives a tiny, one-sentence example
  • It keeps everything digestible at oral speed
  • It fits on one scrollable screen without jargon

The manuscript is complemented by comprehensive course kit files: written notes, slides, transcripts, and a distilled PDF guide, free lifetime updates included.
