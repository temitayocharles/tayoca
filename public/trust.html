Trust Center

Controls that protect evidence, clients and decisions.

Trust is implemented through operating controls, not added as marketing language.

Evidence governance

Claims stay traceable.

Public claims require evidence, review and an explicit approval record.

See the evidence standard
Private by default

Sensitive data stays out.

Repository, client, payment and security data are excluded unless deliberately approved and safely redacted.

Human approval

Automation has boundaries.

Automations may prepare. Sensitive communications and material public publication remain approval-gated.

Buyer review integrity

A review is labelled as a verified buyer review only after the purchase is checked against the commerce provider, the submitted buyer email matches the verified purchase record, publication consent is explicit, and a human publication decision is recorded. Critical sentiment alone is never a rejection criterion.

Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected and recorded. Contact support@tayoca.com.

AI disclosure

AI may assist research, analysis, drafting and automation. Human accountability remains with Tayoca.

Credentials and attestations

Professional credentials, certifications, customer logos and similar attestations are displayed only when a current evidence record and publication approval are retained.

Commercial disclosure

Material sponsorships, affiliate relationships, referral compensation and conflicts of interest are disclosed where they could affect how a recommendation is interpreted.

Generated media

Synthetic or AI-generated imagery is not treated as documentary evidence. When it could reasonably be mistaken for real-world proof, it is labelled as generated, illustrative, simulated or concept media.
