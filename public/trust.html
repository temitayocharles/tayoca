Operating controls

How each control actually works.

Buyer review integrity

A review is labelled as a verified buyer review only after the purchase is checked against the commerce provider, the submitted buyer email matches the verified purchase record, publication consent is explicit, and a human publication decision is recorded. Critical sentiment alone is never a rejection criterion.

Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected and recorded rather than silently rewritten. Contact support@tayoca.com.

AI disclosure

AI may assist research, analysis, drafting and automation. Human accountability remains with Tayoca.

Credentials and attestations

Professional credentials, certifications, customer logos and similar attestations are displayed only when a current evidence record and publication approval are retained.

Commercial disclosure

Material sponsorships, affiliate relationships, referral compensation and conflicts of interest are disclosed where they could affect how a recommendation is interpreted.

Generated media

Synthetic or AI-generated imagery is not treated as documentary evidence. When it could be mistaken for a real customer, deployment or production screenshot, it is labelled as illustrative.

Project disclosure

Existence is not permission.

Tayoca tracks existence, technical nature, source, relationship confidence, disclosure permission, public classification and publication status as separate fields. A project can be real, material and still absent from this website because its disclosure position is unresolved.

Client and community work is not named without consent.

Third-party or jointly-held ventures use neutral classification until ownership is confirmed.

Internal systems are described for context and are not linked as public showcases.

Unresolved items are recorded in the internal inventory — never silently dropped, never silently published.

See how the portfolio is classified →

Security boundaries

Browser-facing code never receives production credentials. Automation credentials are handled server-side, access to operating consoles is restricted, and administrative capability is separated from content operations.

Reported issue handling

Security or evidence concerns are treated as production-incident class: recorded, contained, fixed and then certified with a rollback path. Report them to support@tayoca.com.
