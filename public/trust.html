Operating posture

How the controls run.

Reviews fail closed

Unverified events are never treated as trusted customer evidence. Publication requires verified purchase records and human approval.

Publication is approval-gated

Editorial campaigns, newsletter issues and distribution require human approval before anything reaches the public.

Failures are observable

Production workflows are monitored on a five-minute cycle with sanitized alerts and a documented dead-letter path.
