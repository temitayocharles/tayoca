Operating pressure

AI creates a new operating surface, not just a model choice.

Value depends on the whole workflow: demand, model and tool costs, quality, failure handling, observability and the points where humans must remain accountable.

Cost is difficult to attribute

Model, retrieval, tool and infrastructure usage grow without a useful unit-economic view.

Quality lacks an acceptance boundary

Teams ship workflows without agreed evaluation, fallback or escalation behavior.

Automation hides operational risk

Retries, latency, external dependencies and human handoffs are not treated as first-class production concerns.
