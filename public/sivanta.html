Customer conversations should not become another pile of disconnected manual work.

Sivanta brings selected customer conversations, automation skills, policy controls, checkout and human handoff into one governed flow across channels such as WhatsApp, Telegram and web.

Review plansDiscuss a managed setup

The architecture shown here is a system diagram. Tayoca does not use invented customer screenshots as product evidence.

One governed path instead of a different bot for every channel.

Each customer conversation follows the same operating model even when the entry point changes.

Channel adaptersWhatsApp, Telegram and web interactions are normalized into a shared conversation model.
Skill registryApproved capabilities are registered and versioned instead of being improvised at runtime.
Policy controlsScope, approval and escalation rules apply before an automated action is taken.
CheckoutConversation flows can move into an approved payment path where the configured use case requires it.
Human handoffWhen confidence drops or risk rises, the flow routes to a person rather than pretending automation should decide everything.

Who it is for

Sivanta is intended for teams whose customer conversations, qualification, payments and follow-up are fragmented across channels and require too much manual handling.

Commerce teamsHandle repeatable customer conversations while keeping transaction and escalation rules explicit.
Customer operationsBring selected support, qualification and follow-up flows into one observable operating model.
Growth teamsUse governed conversational workflows without turning each campaign or channel into a separate automation island.
