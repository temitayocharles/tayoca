Community Website Initiative · Nomination

Know a local business that deserves a better website?

You can nominate an Ontario small business that has no website, relies mainly on social media, or has a site that no longer reflects the business well. A nomination simply puts the business on Tayoca's research list. It does not enroll the business or authorize outreach on its behalf.

Nominate a local business See the full initiative

The nomination form does not ask for payment information. Tayoca reviews nominations before deciding whether a business is a fit for the initiative.

How it works
01
You nominate

Tell us the business name, community, how you know it, why a stronger website could help, and any public contact information you already know.

02
Tayoca researches first

We verify that the business is active, examine its current web presence, and check whether a website project would be useful before any approach is considered.

03
The business keeps control

A nomination is not an application and does not commit the business to anything. If Tayoca reaches out, the business can simply decline.
