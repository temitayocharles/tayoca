01 · The free core

A useful website, not a decorative placeholder.

Scope is deliberately practical. The goal is to give a selected business a credible, maintainable starting point it can actually own.

01
Business-specific UX and visual direction

Research into your business, customers, local context and current web presence before design begins.

02
Responsive essential pages

A polished mobile and desktop experience covering the pages your business genuinely needs.

03
Clear customer action

Contact, quote, booking, call, visit or another primary conversion path appropriate to the business.

04
Technical foundations

Basic technical SEO, sensible metadata, accessibility-minded implementation and analytics-ready setup.

05
Preview and source handoff

A staging preview for review, then repository/source handoff so the business is not locked out of its own website.

05 · Common questions

Free website help for local Ontario businesses.

The initiative is designed for small businesses that need a credible web presence without turning the free build into a sales trap.

01
Which Ontario communities are included?

Shelburne, Orangeville, Alliston, Dufferin County, Simcoe County and nearby Ontario communities are part of the first-round focus.

02
Is the website core build really free?

Yes. Selected businesses pay $0 for the agreed core build. Hosting, domains, maintenance, e-commerce or other optional services are separate and never required to receive the promised core build.

03
Why does the application open in Google Forms?

It gives applicants a familiar, recognizable application surface. Tayoca.com remains the canonical initiative page and the place to understand eligibility, scope and process.

04
What happens after I apply?

Tayoca reviews fit and available capacity first. Approved applications then move into business research, website planning, build, review and source handoff.
