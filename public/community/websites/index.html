What the free core build includes

The website starts with the business, not a generic Tayoca template. We research what you do, who you serve and what a useful visitor action should be before the visual direction is chosen.

Business-specific directionResearch into your business, customers, local context and current online presence before design starts.
Essential responsive pagesA polished mobile and desktop experience covering the pages the business genuinely needs.
A clear customer actionContact, call, quote, booking, visit or another primary conversion path appropriate to the business.
Technical foundationsBasic technical SEO, sensible metadata, accessibility-minded implementation and analytics-ready setup.
Preview and handoffA staging preview for review, followed by source/repository handoff so the business is not locked out of its own website.

Free should mean free.

If you are selected, the promised core website build does not become conditional on buying something else.

Optional items are separate conversations.

  • Domain or long-term hosting costs
  • Secure owner CMS or /admin capability
  • Ongoing maintenance and security updates
  • Managed local SEO and reporting
  • E-commerce or custom application features
  • Booking, payment, CRM or automation systems

You can decline every optional item above and still receive the agreed free core build.
