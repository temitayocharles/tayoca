The operating loop

Every engagement runs the same five movements.

The loop is the reason a Tayoca engagement ends with something the client can operate, not just a document.

Engagement model

Commercial terms follow the evidence boundary.

Fixed scope is preferred where inputs and acceptance criteria can be bounded. Larger implementations use milestones; managed work starts only after a measurable baseline and service boundary exist.

Diagnose first

A fit call or assessment identifies the decision and evidence needed before implementation expands.

Explicit scope

Inclusions, exclusions, acceptance criteria and change control are written down.

Evidence-backed outcomes

Estimates and modeled savings state assumptions; realized results require validation.

No hidden guarantees

Refunds, service levels, timing and performance commitments exist only when their conditions are in the approved scope.

The boundary

What Tayoca will and will not claim.

Savings are presented as modeled or estimated until they are implemented and measured against an agreed baseline.

Delivery timelines are tied to agreed milestones, not to optimistic best cases.

Service-level commitments apply only where they are written into the signed scope.

AI and automation work ships with defined failure behaviour, observability and a named human escalation path.

Case studies and quantified outcomes are published only after evidence, disclosure, approval and scope checks.
