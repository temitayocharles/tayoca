What happens after you contact us

We keep the process simple enough that you can see where the work is, what decision is being made and what should be true before the next step.

1. UnderstandWe clarify the problem, the decision you need to make and the evidence that already exists.
2. Bound itWe agree the inputs, exclusions, acceptance criteria and smallest useful piece of work.
3. Change itWe implement the agreed intervention with the controls, observability and handoff the work needs.
4. Verify itWe compare the result with the baseline and document what improved, what did not and what should happen next.

What you should expect from us

We tell you when a saving or improvement is still an estimate rather than a verified result.

Scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria and change control are written down before the work expands.

Delivery timelines and service commitments only apply when they are part of the agreed engagement.

Automation work includes observable failure behaviour and a human escalation path where the workflow needs one.

Handover includes the information your team needs to own what was built.
