About · The company

An engineering-led company that builds, operates and teaches.

Tayoca is a technology company, not a single consulting funnel. Its work spans client delivery, owned software, publications, a newsletter and community programmes — all operating under one evidence standard.

01 / Who we are

A builder's company.

Tayoca was founded by Temitayo Charles — an engineer, writer and builder — to make complex technology reliable, economically accountable, secure and operationally scalable. That sentence is not a slogan; it is the operating contract behind every service, product and publication on this site.

The company does its own engineering on its own systems. It runs a real operating stack behind the public site — intake, editorial, newsletter, trust and revenue workflows with human approval gates — and it publishes the playbooks and briefings it learns from. What visitors see is not a marketing facade; it is the front door of a working platform.

04 / How we operate

Evidence is the culture, not the compliance department.

Tayoca's public standard is simple: claims carry a baseline, an intervention record, a measurement method, known limits and an approval. That is why the Results Standard exists, why a previously published quantified case narrative is withdrawn from proof use while its evidence is re-verified, and why reviews are purchase-verified and human-approved before publication.

This discipline also powers the internal system: editorial campaigns need human approval before anything is published; corrections are recorded as revisions, never silent rewrites; failures are observable through a monitored dead-letter path.
