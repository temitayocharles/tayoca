Why Tayoca exists

Tayoca was founded by Temitayo Charles, an engineer, writer and builder based in Ontario, Canada.

The company grew from practical engineering work into several connected forms: client delivery, operator playbooks, owned software, editorial work and community projects. They are different outputs, but they are tied together by a simple idea: technology should be understandable enough to operate, justify and improve.

That means looking at cost as seriously as architecture, recovery as seriously as deployment, and day-two ownership as seriously as the first launch.

How we try to work

The commitments below apply whether the job is an assessment, an implementation, a software product or something we publish.

Show the evidenceFacts, estimates and recommendations stay separate. A modeled saving is still a model until it is implemented and measured.
Keep the scope visibleInputs, exclusions, ownership and acceptance criteria should be understandable before the work expands.
Keep people accountableAutomation can assist analysis and execution, but sensitive decisions and material publication remain accountable to people.
Design for day twoRunbooks, observability, recovery behaviour and ownership are part of a useful handover, not optional cleanup after the build.

How the company grew

It did not begin as a catalogue of unrelated offers. Each part grew out of work that already existed.
