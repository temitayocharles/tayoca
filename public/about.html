01 / Who we are

A builder's company.

Tayoca was founded by Temitayo Charles — an engineer, writer and builder — to make complex technology reliable, economically accountable, secure and operationally scalable. That sentence is not a slogan; it is the operating contract behind every service, product and publication on this site.

The company runs its own engineering on its own systems. Behind this public site sits a real operating stack: intake, editorial, newsletter, trust, revenue and project-intelligence workflows with human approval gates, revision history and recovery controls. What visitors see is the front door of a working platform, not a brochure.

Because the same team writes the playbooks it sells, teaches from the incidents it has handled and operates the software it ships, the material on this site carries a provenance most marketing sites cannot claim.

02 / Operating principles

What working with Tayoca should feel like.

Four commitments that apply whether the engagement is a two-week assessment or a managed operating capability.

Evidence before claims

Facts, estimates and recommendations stay separated.

A modeled saving is labelled as modeled until it is implemented and measured against an agreed baseline.

Bounded scope

Acceptance criteria come first.

Exclusions, ownership and change control are explicit before implementation expands.

Human accountability

Automation assists; people answer.

AI can support analysis and execution, but material publication and sensitive decisions remain accountable to people.

Operable handover

Day two is part of the deliverable.

Runbooks, observability, failure behaviour and named ownership matter as much as the initial build.

On credentials and claims

Professional credentials are not displayed on this site unless a current verification record exists and the claim is one Tayoca is prepared to evidence. Where a previously published quantified outcome narrative is under re-verification, it is withdrawn from proof use rather than quietly repeated.

Baseline, intervention record, measurement method, known limits and approval are required before a result is published.

Reviews are relationship-verified and published only with explicit consent.

Corrections create a preserved revision or public notice — never a silent rewrite.
