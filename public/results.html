Results · Evidence standard

Proof before promises.

Tayoca connects claims to implementation evidence, operating records or approved outcomes — and keeps the standard visible instead of burying it in fine print.

01 / The standard

What a credible result includes.

What Tayoca shows

  • A stated baseline and the period it covers
  • The intervention and who did what
  • The measurement method and its limitations
  • Known assumptions and what would change the conclusion
  • The approval recorded before publication

What Tayoca avoids

  • Unsupported percentages and hidden assumptions
  • Guaranteed outcomes and claims that cannot be reproduced
  • Treating modeled savings as realized results
  • Silent rewrites of published claims — corrections are revisions
