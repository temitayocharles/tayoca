Evidence standard

What a credible result includes.

What Tayoca shows

Baseline, intervention, validation, operating evidence, measurement period and known limits.

What Tayoca avoids

Unsupported percentages, hidden assumptions, unapproved client attribution, guaranteed outcomes and claims that cannot be re-verified.

Withdrawn narrative

A previously published quantified AWS case-study narrative is withdrawn from proof use while its evidence is re-verified. It is not used as a headline result, a proof statistic, or a sales guarantee anywhere on this site.

The four checks

Before anything becomes public proof.

1 · Evidence

Traceable source

A baseline and an intervention record exist in the originating system.

2 · Disclosure

Permission on file

Naming, attribution and any client or community reference are consented.

3 · Approval

Human decision

A named human approved publication; automation may prepare but not publish.

4 · Scope

Bounded claim

The claim states its measurement period, assumptions and known limits.
