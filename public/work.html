Sivanta

Sivanta is conversation-led commerce software for teams that need governed flows across messaging and web channels, with policy controls, checkout and a clear path to human handoff.

It is an owned Tayoca product, so the work includes product decisions, operating boundaries, pricing, support and the consequences of keeping it running.

SiteSupply

SiteSupply is a construction marketplace that helps buyers compare suppliers rather than being locked into a single vendor relationship. Suppliers get another route to prospective buyers, while buyers get more choice when they need materials or services.

Free Website Initiative

A community programme providing practical website core builds for selected local small businesses and organizations in Ontario. The purpose is straightforward: help people get a useful online presence without making the free build conditional on buying another service.
