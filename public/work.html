06 / Classification

How Tayoca decides what may appear here.

Existence, ownership confidence, disclosure permission and publication status are tracked separately. Something can be real and still be absent from this page.

Step 1

Existence

Is there verifiable evidence the project exists — source, a live surface, or a canonical internal record?

Step 2

Nature & relationship

What kind of thing is it, and what is Tayoca's actual relationship to it: owned, built, advised, contributed, internal?

Step 3

Disclosure permission

Third-party, client and community work needs consent. Without it the item stays out of the public IA regardless of merit.

Step 4

Public classification

Only then is a public label chosen: owned product, venture build, publication, community programme or internal system.

Step 5

Publication status

Published, staged, or recorded-not-published. The distinction is visible on the page, not hidden in a spreadsheet.

Recorded, not published

Some real Tayoca-adjacent projects are held in the internal company inventory while ownership or disclosure is confirmed. Holding an item back is a deliberate classification decision — an unresolved item is never silently dropped, and never silently published either.
